# Product Guidelines: Charity Draws

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- **Visual Style:** Modern, clean, and high-trust. Combines vibrant gamified elements (draws, instant wins, countdowns) with solid non-profit credibility and security.
- **Color Palette & Design System:** Built on Tailwind CSS v4 with dark/light mode balance, crisp typography, subtle elevation borders, and prominent CTA accents.
- **Iconography & Visuals:** Crisp Lucide React icons, high-resolution prize imagery, and transparent status badges.

## Tone of Voice & Communication
- **Trustworthy & Transparent:** Clear odds, visible ticket numbers, transparent fee structures, and explicit draw rules. Avoid deceptive hype or ambiguous fine print.
- **Encouraging & Mission-Driven:** Highlight the charitable impact of causes and celebrate winners with respect and excitement.
- **Concise & Direct:** User-facing notices, form feedback, and error messages should be actionable, concise, and informative.

## UX Principles & Standards
- **Frictionless Entry:** Streamlined ticket selection and fast checkout with clear instant-win allocation displays.
- **Real-Time Responsiveness:** TanStack Query cache synchronization for real-time ticket counts, countdowns, and host balance updates.
- **Mobile-First Accessibility:** Smooth mobile bottom docks, responsive filter sheets, touch-friendly buttons, and horizontal table scrolling.
- **Comprehensive Feedback:** Sonner toast alerts, inline validation errors, and clear loading indicators on every async mutation.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Prominent display of verified host credentials, age restrictions, and free postal entry alternatives.
